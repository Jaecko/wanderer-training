# Wanderer Training

What your trainers have for you, and what it will cost. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- Its own window (opened from Wanderer's bar and menu, a button on your spellbook, a key of yours or /wtraining): your class spells, weapon skills and demon tomes, in sections: to learn now, an earlier rank first, level by level, with a talent, set aside.
- Three sums to plan ahead: now, at the next level that brings something, and everything left; your gold beside them. Prices with your reputation's discount.
- What you play counts: choose your specialization (several at once, the bear and the cat apart for a feral druid). Spells of another one stay shown, can be bought, and counted if you wish: it guides, it never blocks.
- Your talents planned point by point, as the game's rules allow, the next one said at each level.
- The closest class trainer or weapon master pinned on your map; at the trainer, what counts learned in one click.
- The prices your trainer really asks, kept once seen there; a savings goal of your own beside the sums (riding at level 40 in one click).
- In your spell tooltips, the next rank: its level and its price, and for a spell outside what you play, why in one line. Older ranks on your bars and in your macros updated in one click.

Built on the data of [What's Training](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/whats-training) (MIT), with trainer places from CMaNGOS (GPL v3) and pfQuest (MIT); see CREDITS.md. Free software under the GNU GPL v3 or later.
