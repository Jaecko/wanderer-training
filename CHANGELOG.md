# Wanderer Training 1.4.12

### New
- Talent calculator: the points of a plan can be moved in the order list, dragged to another level or one level at a time with their arrows; a move that would break the tree's rules is refused, with the reason.

### Changed
- The talent planner's level steps with a minus and a plus; Count again shows an undo sign; Rename a goal shows a pencil.
- Shift+click on a spell in the Training page or at the trainer links it in the chat.
- The window is titled Training and plays its open and close sounds.
- Talent order rows beyond the level you look at are easier to read.
- At every trainer (professions, weapons, riding), anything can be left out like your class's spells: right click or its ..., Leave out; it is then never bought with the others.

### Fixed
- At a hunter's pet trainer, prices are the pet's training points (as the game shows them), never gold, and learn all goes as far as the points go.
- Learning a new profession asks first, in the game's own words.
- At a profession trainer, your rank shows beside the trainer's name.
- The Calculator button on the talent window is hidden while Wanderer Training is off.
- At a trainer, a spell held back says the right reason: an earlier rank only when it is one, the skill named.
- Moving a point of a talent plan moves the one its row shows.
