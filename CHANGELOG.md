# Wanderer Training 1.4.3

### New
- Controller support: the talent calculator takes the controller and starts on Simulate; the triggers switch to the game's talent tree and back, and the right button closes the game's talents with the calculator. Needs Wanderer 1.4.36 or later.

### Fixed
- Talents bought at the trainer no longer leave the trainer's buttons disabled.
- A stray highlight in the talent calculator is gone.
